FOUR WAYS TO CONNECT

Hook up however you like.

Personal Hotspot

One phone hosts, everyone else scans its code in the app — no settings, no password. Then just talk, hands-free.

Bluetooth Link

No signal? Link two phones straight over Bluetooth. Your friend shows up by name, connects on its own, and reconnects next time you open the app.

Android & iPhone

Different phones? One Android shares the connection, the iPhone joins with a quick scan. Clear voice the whole way.

Join via Browser

No app? Friends jump in from Safari or Chrome. Send a link or let them scan your code — even over the internet.

  • 16 kHz voice, Opus-coded — a small packet every 20 ms

WHAT IT ASKS FOR

  • The microphone. That one's the whole point.
  • Nearby devices — only when you use Bluetooth or the hotspot bridge.
  • Location on older Androids, because Android won't scan for Wi-Fi or Bluetooth without it.
  • Notifications, so the channel keeps running with the screen off and the phone in a pocket.

WHAT IT NEVER ASKS FOR

  • An account, a phone number or an email. There's no sign-up screen to skip.
  • A server in the middle. Voice goes phone to phone; a browser guest only borrows a public STUN server to find you, never to carry the audio.
  • Ads, trackers or analytics. None are in the app.
  • Storage for your voice. Audio is played and gone — nothing is recorded.
