ACCOUNT DELETION

How to delete your Tarkk account.

The quickest way is inside the app. If you can’t use the app, you can do it right here: we email a one-time link to the account’s address, and you confirm on the page it opens. Either way it takes effect at once.

WHAT IS DELETED

  • Your email address, and your password hash or the link to your Google account.
  • Your display name and avatar choice.
  • Your sign-in sessions. Every phone signed in to the account is signed out.
  • Your purchase and subscription records. A subscription still running in Bazaar can be restored into a new account.

WHAT MAY BE KEPT, AND WHY

  • Security records, such as failed sign-ins, for up to a year — with your account id removed, and the IP address only as a one-way hash.
  • Cafe Bazaar’s own record of your payment, which Bazaar keeps under its own policy.
  • A subscription in Bazaar keeps running until you cancel it there. Deleting the account doesn’t cancel or refund it.
  • What’s on your phone. Tarkk keeps working without an account, with your channels, name and settings.

Delete it in the app Link to this section

This is the fastest way, and it happens straight away.

  • Open Tarkk and go to Profile.
  • In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  • Confirm it’s you: type the account’s email and your password — or, for a Google account, sign in with Google again.
  • If a subscription is still running, the app reminds you that deleting the account doesn’t cancel it.
  • Confirm once more. The account is deleted, and every phone signed in to it is signed out.
THERE IS NO UNDO

A deleted account can’t be brought back. If you make a new account later, it starts empty — though a subscription that is still running in Bazaar can be restored into it.

Delete it here, on the website Link to this section

No phone, a forgotten password, or a Google account you no longer use? Enter the account’s email below. If it belongs to a Tarkk account, we email it a one-time link that works for 15 minutes. The link opens a page that shows what will be deleted and asks you to confirm.

Only someone who can open this inbox can delete the account. We never say whether an email has an account.

Check your inbox

Nothing yet? Look in your spam folder.

If you no longer have access to that inbox either, write to tarkk.hp@gmail.com from any address and tell us what you can about the account. We will ask for proof that it’s yours before deleting anything.

What is deleted, and what may be kept Link to this section

Deleting the account removes these from our server:

  • Your email address.
  • Your password hash, for a password account, or your Google account id, for a Google account.
  • Your display name and avatar id.
  • Your sign-in sessions, including each phone’s platform and install key.
  • Your purchase and subscription records.

Security records — for example a failed sign-in, or the fact that an account was deleted — stay for up to a year so we can look into abuse. Your account id is removed from them, and an IP address is only ever stored as a one-way hash. Cafe Bazaar keeps its own record of a payment, under Bazaar’s policy; we can’t delete that for you.

There is nothing about your conversations to delete, because none of it was ever on our server: voice, presence and what is said in a channel never leave the local link.

What the app keeps on your phone stays there — Tarkk keeps working without an account. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Your subscription Link to this section

CANCEL IT IN BAZAAR

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Cafe Bazaar, so cancel it in Bazaar as well, or it will keep renewing. Deleting the account doesn’t refund it either.
